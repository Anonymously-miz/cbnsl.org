Youth Skills Programme Launches in Bo — 300 Trainees Enrolled

📅 12 March 2026✍ By Ibrahim Sesay⏱ 2 min read

300 young Sierra Leoneans will receive vocational training in 2026.

Our newest flagship initiative kicked off this month at the CBN Skills Hub in Bo. Over the next six months, 120 young people will train in carpentry, tailoring, ICT and agri-business, with a second cohort of 180 starting in August — including the first trainees at our new hub in Kenema.

Every trainee also receives business skills, financial literacy and a starter tool-kit — and the top 20% of each cohort win a $300 business start-up grant to launch their own enterprise. Within three months, our first ICT trainees had already earned Le 8.4 million from freelance digital work, and the tailoring class had sold 240 garments at Bo market.

"I was selling mobile phone credit on the street for two years after I left school," says Mohamed Kamara, 22, a graduate of last year's pilot. "CBN gave me six months of tailoring training and a sewing machine. Now I have my own shop and I employ three of my friends from the programme."
