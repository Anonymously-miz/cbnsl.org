About Community Builders Network

We are a Sierra Leonean-led NGO working with communities, government and partners to build a fairer, healthier and more prosperous Sierra Leone.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

What we do, and the future we're working to create alongside the communities of Sierra Leone.

M

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable communities across Sierra Leone by delivering inclusive programmes in education, health, clean water, livelihoods and youth leadership — building local capacity so that change is owned, sustained and led by Sierra Leoneans themselves.

V

Our Vision

A Sierra Leone where every child, woman and man can live with dignity, safety and opportunity — a nation of resilient communities that shape their own future free from poverty, injustice and preventable illness.

Our Journey

History & Achievements

Fifteen years of milestones — from a small volunteer collective in Freetown to a nationwide community development network.

2010

CBN Founded

Community Builders Network is registered as a Sierra Leonean NGO by a group of young graduates in Freetown.

2014

Ebola Emergency Response

Deployed 200 community health mobilisers during the outbreak, reaching more than 40,000 households with life-saving information.

2018

Girls' Education Programme

Launched the flagship scholarship scheme that has kept 1,500+ girls in school across three districts.

2021

Nationwide Reach

Expanded operations to all 16 districts of Sierra Leone with a network of 320 community facilitators.

2025

Recognised Nationally

Awarded the Sierra Leone Presidential Award for Excellence in Community Development.
